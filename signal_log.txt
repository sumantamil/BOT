================================================================================
  STRATEGY SIGNAL REFERENCE  (what to look for in the dashboard)
================================================================================

  📋 PAPER ORB   : Opening Range Breakout  |  Window: 9:15–9:30  |  Entry: 9:30–12:30
     Look for    : "📋 PAPER ORB: Would buy CE/PE | strength XX%"
     Record      : Entry price = range high/low + buffer  |  SL = other end of range
     Target T1   : entry ± (range_width × 2.5)
     Target T2   : entry ± (range_width × 3.5)

  📋 PAPER VWAP  : VWAP Mean Reversion  |  Window: 9:30–14:30  |  RANGING regime only
     Look for    : "📋 PAPER VWAP: Would buy CE/PE | deviation XX%"
     Record      : Entry = current price  |  Target = VWAP level
     Trigger     : Price > 0.5% below/above VWAP  AND  RSI < 40 (LONG) / > 60 (SHORT)

  📋 PAPER TREND : 5m Multi-Indicator Trend  |  Any time before 14:00
     Look for    : "📋 PAPER TREND [NIFTY/BANKNIFTY/SENSEX]: Would buy CE/PE"
     Record      : Entry = current spot price  |  SL = 15%  |  Target = 30%

  📋 PAPER GAP   : Gap open trade  |  Window: 9:15–11:00
     Look for    : "Gap Trade Executed" or gap report with direction
     Record      : Entry = ATM at open  |  SL = 25%  |  Target = 50%
